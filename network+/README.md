# NetworkAcademy+ 🖧

**A complete, beginner-to-certified training program for the CompTIA Network+ (N10-009) exam — built for someone starting with zero computer knowledge.**

Welcome. This is not a pile of notes. It is a structured, commercial-quality training academy — the kind you would expect from CompTIA, the Cisco Networking Academy, or Microsoft Learn — designed around one promise:

> **If you can read, you can pass Network+ and build a real home network. We will not skip a single step.**

---

## Who this is for

Someone who has **never** configured a network, may be new to computers in general, and learns best by **reading**, **seeing diagrams**, **looking at real photos**, **following step-by-step instructions**, and **doing hands-on practice**.

Everything here is written at about a **7th-grade reading level**, always explains **WHY before HOW**, and never assumes you already know something.

## What you will be able to do when you finish

1. **Pass the CompTIA Network+ (N10-009) certification exam.**
2. **Build and operate a real home lab** using your own equipment.

## The equipment you will use

| Gear | Role in this course |
|------|--------------------|
| Windows laptop | Your workstation, terminal, and testing tool |
| Dell PowerConnect 3424 (managed switch) | Your hands-on switching lab — VLANs, ports, management |
| Home router | Your gateway to the internet and DHCP/DNS lab |
| Ethernet cables | The physical layer you will build and test |

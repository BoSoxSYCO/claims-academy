# [Module 00 · Lesson 02] How This Academy Works

> **Unit:** Unit 1 — On-Ramp · **Time:** ~20 min · **Equipment:** none
> **You'll need first:** just Lesson 00-01.

You know what this course is about. Now let's learn how to use it. Every good tool comes with a simple guide, and this is yours. Once you know how the pieces fit, the whole course feels easy to follow. There is nothing to memorize here — just a friendly tour of how things are laid out.

---

## 1. Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

- [ ] Name the main parts of the course and what each one is for.
- [ ] Describe the shape every lesson follows.
- [ ] Follow a simple routine to work through one lesson.
- [ ] Know where to look when a word is fuzzy.

---

## 2. Introduction — why this matters

A course you understand is a course you'll finish. When the layout is a mystery, you waste energy wondering "what do I do next?" This lesson removes that.

The course is built the same way all the way through. Same lesson shape. Same kinds of practice. Same place to look up words. Once you learn the pattern once, it never changes on you.

That sameness is on purpose. It lets you spend your energy learning networks, not learning the course.

🎯 **Exam tip:** The more you use the practice parts — workbooks, quizzes, flashcards — the readier you'll be. They are your best friends here.

🔧 **Lab link:** Some lessons include a lab, where you do things with real equipment. We'll tour your gear in Lesson 00-04.

## 3. Simple explanation

Let's meet the parts of the course, then a routine for using them.

### How the course is organized

- The course is split into modules — numbered 00 through 15. Each module covers one big topic.
- Modules are grouped into units (also called phases), which build on each other in order.
- Each module holds several lessons. You do them in order, one at a time.

### The shape of every lesson

Every lesson follows the same seven-part path. Once you know it, you always know what's coming:

1. Why it matters — the real-world reason first.
2. Key words — every new term, defined plainly.
3. The idea — explained simply, with a diagram.
4. See it for real — a photo or screenshot.
5. Step by step — do-it-yourself instructions.
6. Check yourself — a few quick questions.
7. Remember this — the key takeaways.

### The practice parts

- A workbook — printable worksheets to practice each lesson.
- A quiz — a friendly knowledge check; the answers live in a separate answer key.
- A cheat sheet — a one-page summary you can print.
- A glossary — every term in the course, listed alphabetically, each defined in plain words.
- Labs — hands-on activities with real equipment.
- Near the end: two full practice exams and a set of flashcards to get you exam-ready.

### A simple routine for one lesson

Do this for each lesson, and you can't go wrong:

1. **Read** the lesson from top to bottom.
2. **Look** at the diagram until it makes sense.
3. **Do** the workbook worksheets.
4. **Take** the quiz, then check the answer key.
5. **Keep** the cheat sheet nearby for quick review.

> **The big idea:** The course is modules made of lessons, and every lesson has the same shape. Read it, look at the diagram, do the workbook, take the quiz, and use the cheat sheet and glossary. Same pattern every time.

💡 **Tip:** When a word feels fuzzy, stop and look it up in the glossary. That habit alone will carry you far.

⚠️ **Watch out:** Don't skip ahead. Each lesson leans on the ones before it. In order is the easy way.

## 9. Hands-on lab

**Goal:** take a short tour of the course folders so you know where things live.
**Why:** knowing where to find each part makes every later lesson smoother.

**You will need**
- This course open on a screen (or printed), and about 10 minutes.

⚠️ **Before you start:** you are only looking around. Nothing to change or break.

### 9a. Step-by-step instructions

1. Open Module 00 and list the lessons you see (00-01, 00-02, and so on).
2. Open one lesson and find its seven parts (why it matters, key words, and the rest).
3. Find that lesson's workbook worksheets and its quiz.
4. Open the glossary and look up one word — try "network."
5. Open a cheat sheet and read it in under a minute.

### 9b. Expected results

- You've seen a lesson, a workbook, a quiz, the glossary, and a cheat sheet with your own eyes.

- ✅ **You did it if:** you can say where each part lives and what it's for.

> ⚠️ Folder names may look technical at first. That's fine — you'll get comfortable fast.

### 9c. Verify it worked

1. Can you point to where the lessons are?
2. Can you find a term in the glossary?

### 9d. Reset / roll back

Nothing to undo — you only explored. Now you know your way around.

> 🔧 **Home-lab continuity:** the "labs" folders are where the hands-on gear activities live; you'll use them from Module 00-04 on.

## 10. Common mistakes

| ⚠️ Mistake | What happens | How to fix / avoid it |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------------|
| Skipping lessons | Later ideas won't make sense | Do them in order |
| Ignoring the workbook | The ideas fade fast | Practice cements learning |
| Never checking the glossary | Fuzzy words pile up | Look up any unclear term right away |
| Reading the quiz answers first | You skip the thinking | Answer first, then check the key |

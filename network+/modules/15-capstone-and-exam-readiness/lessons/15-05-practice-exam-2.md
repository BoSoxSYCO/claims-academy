# [Module 15 · Lesson 05] Full-Length Practice Exam #2 + Review

> **Unit:** Unit 12 — Capstone & Exam Readiness · **Time:** ~120 min (90 to test + 30 to compare) · **Equipment:** E1
> **You'll need first:** Lesson 15-04 (your Exam #1 score and weak-domain list).

You took one practice exam and found your weak spots. Now let's measure again with a fresh exam. One score is a snapshot. Two scores show a trend. This lesson is about comparing Exam #2 to Exam #1 — proving your review worked, and spotting any domain that still lags. That trend tells you if you are ready.

---

## 1. Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

- [ ] Take a second timed 90-question exam under real conditions.
- [ ] Compare your two scores, both overall and per domain.
- [ ] Confirm which weak spots are closing and which remain.
- [ ] Decide if you are ready, using a clear "ready signal."

---

## 2. Introduction — why this matters

A single score can fool you. You might have a good day or a lucky guess. Two scores across two different exams are much harder to fake. The trend is the truth.

Exam #2 is a fresh form. The questions differ from Exam #1, but they cover the same five domains. So if a domain improved, your review is working. If a domain still lags, you know exactly where to spend your last study days.

This is also your readiness check. Two solid scores in a row is the green light. One good, one shaky means keep going.

🎯 **Exam tip:** Take Exam #2 at least a day or two after Exam #1 — spacing makes the retest honest.

🔧 **Lab link:** Bring your Exam #1 weak-domain list. You'll compare it side by side.

---

## 3. Simple explanation

Same three phases as before — **Take**, **Score**, **Compare** — but the last one changes from "review one" to "compare two."

### Phase 1 — Take (90 minutes)

- Open `assessments/practice-exams/practice-exam-02.md`.
- Set a 90-minute timer. No notes, no phone, no blanks.
- Flag and return. Answer PBQ-style items fully.

### Phase 2 — Score

- Use `instructor/answer-keys/practice-exam-02-answers.md`.
- Count correct out of 90. Percentage = correct ÷ 90.

### Phase 3 — Compare (the new part)

- Put Exam #1 and Exam #2 side by side.
- Compare the overall score first — did it rise?
- Then compare each domain's misses. Circle any domain still weak.
- Update your weak-domain log for **Lesson 15-06**.

### The ready signal

- **Ready:** 80%+ on both exams, with no domain badly behind.
- **Almost:** one exam 80%+, one a bit lower — drill the lagging domain.
- **Not yet:** either exam under 70% — review those modules before you book.

> **The big idea:** One score is a snapshot; two scores are a trend. If your weak domains improved from #1 to #2, your review is working — keep the pattern until both exams clear 80%.

💡 **Tip:** Improvement matters as much as the raw number. A jump from 60% to 78% is real progress.

⚠️ **Watch out:** Don't retake the same exam from memory. Use the second form so the score is honest.

## 4. Real-world analogy

> **"Two weigh-ins beat one — the trend tells the story."**

| One weigh-in | Two weigh-ins |
|--------------|---------------|
| A single number | A direction (up or down) |
| Could be a fluke | Harder to fake |
| No plan | Shows what's working |
| Guessing | Evidence |

A coach never judges progress on one measurement. They compare two and watch the trend.

## 9. Hands-on lab

**Goal:** take Exam #2 timed, then build a two-exam comparison to judge readiness.
**Why:** the comparison turns two scores into a clear go / keep-studying decision.

**You will need**
- Exam #2, its key, your Exam #1 tally, a 90-minute timer, and the progress tracker. ~120 min.

⚠️ **Before you start:** use the second form, not Exam #1 from memory. No notes; no key until the timer ends.

### 9a. Step-by-step instructions

1. Open `assessments/practice-exams/practice-exam-02.md`.
2. Set a 90-minute timer. Begin. Leave no blanks.
3. When time ends, score with the answer key.
4. Write your Exam #2 score next to your Exam #1 score on `wb-15-05-hands-on-observation.md`.
5. For each domain, write #1 misses and #2 misses side by side.
6. Mark each domain "improved," "same," or "worse."
7. Read the ready signal and circle your result.

### 9b. Expected results

- A two-exam comparison table, an overall trend, and a ready / keep-studying decision.

- ✅ **You did it if:** you have both scores, a per-domain comparison, and a clear readiness call.

### 9c. Verify it worked

1. Did your overall score rise, or hold at 80%+?
2. Did your previous weak domains improve?

### 9d. Reset / roll back

Keep both tallies. Carry any still-weak domain into Lesson 15-06.

## 10. Common mistakes

| ⚠️ Mistake | What happens | How to fix / avoid it |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------------|
| Retaking Exam #1 from memory | A fake-high score | Use the second form (Exam #2) |
| Only comparing overall % | You miss a weak domain | Compare per domain too |
| Taking both the same day | Fatigue skews #2 | Space them a day or two apart |
| Ignoring a lagging domain | It follows you to the real exam | Drill it before you book |

---

## 11. Troubleshooting

| The result | What it means / do next |
|-----------|--------------------------|
| Both 80%+, even domains | Strong — you're likely ready |
| Overall rose, one domain still low | Drill that one domain for 15-06 |
| Score dropped from #1 to #2 | Normal if #2 felt harder; review misses and retest |
| Both under 70% | Re-study weak modules before booking the exam |

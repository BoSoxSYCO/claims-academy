# [Module 15 · Lesson 07] Your 7-Day Pre-Exam Plan

> **Unit:** Unit 12 — Capstone & Exam Readiness · **Time:** ~30 min · **Equipment:** E1 (none; your exams, flashcards, logs)
> **You'll need first:** the whole of Module 15 — both practice exams, your weak-domain logs, and the core flashcards.

This is the last lesson of the whole course. You have the knowledge, the practice exams, and the flashcards. Now you need a plan for the final week. A good plan removes panic. It tells you exactly what to do each day, so you never wonder "am I doing enough?" Follow it, and you'll walk in calm and ready.

---

## 1. Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

- [ ] Follow a day-by-day plan for the last week before the exam.
- [ ] Balance review, practice exams, and rest.
- [ ] Do a full logistics check before exam day.
- [ ] Walk in with a calm, tested game plan.

---

## 2. Introduction — why this matters

The week before a big test is where good preparation is won or lost. Cram too hard and you burn out. Do too little and gaps stay open. The fix is a simple, paced plan.

You already have every tool. Two practice exams. A weak-domain log. A flashcard deck. A troubleshooting method for life. This lesson just puts them in the right order across seven days.

The goal for the final week is not to learn new things. It is to sharpen what you know, close your last gaps, and arrive rested.

🎯 **Exam tip:** The day before the exam is for rest and light review — never a heavy cram.

🔧 **Lab link:** Your capstone lab, both exams, and the flashcards are the tools this plan uses.

---

## 3. Simple explanation

Here is the plan, counting down from seven days out. Adjust the dates to your exam day.

### Day 7 — Diagnose

- Take **Practice Exam #1** timed, if you haven't (Lesson 15-04).
- Score it. List your weak domains, most misses first.
- This is your map for the week.

### Day 6 — Drill weak domains

- Flashcard sprints on your weakest domain (active recall, spaced).
- Re-read only the exact topics you missed — not whole modules.

### Day 5 — Drill the next weak domain

- Same method on your second-weakest domain.
- Re-drill yesterday's "review again" pile (spaced repetition).

### Day 4 — Re-measure

- Take **Practice Exam #2** timed (Lesson 15-05).
- Compare to Exam #1, per domain. Confirm weak spots are closing.

### Day 3 — Close the gaps + PBQ practice

- Drill any domain still weak after Exam #2.
- Do a round of **PBQ practice** (Lesson 15-03): read, plan, do, guess, check.

### Day 2 — Light full pass + logistics

- One light flashcard pass across all domains (no heavy cram).
- Do your logistics check (see Section 5's list): ID, voucher, location/route or online-proctor setup, and time.

### Day 1 — Rest and prep

- Light review only. A short flashcard skim at most.
- Pack your ID and voucher info. Plan your route or test your webcam.
- Sleep well. A rested brain beats a crammed one.

### Exam Day — Execute

- Arrive early (or set up your proctoring space early).
- Do a quick brain dump of facts you fear forgetting.
- Flag PBQs, bank the fast multiple-choice, never leave a blank, watch the clock.

> **The big idea:** The final week sharpens, it does not cram. Diagnose, drill your weak domains, re-measure, close gaps, check logistics, rest — then execute the game plan on exam day.

💡 **Tip:** Write your plan on a calendar with real dates. A plan you can see is a plan you'll follow.

⚠️ **Watch out:** Don't move your exam later just because you feel nervous. If your practice scores are ready, trust them.

## 4. Real-world analogy

> **"The final week is a race taper, not a last sprint."**

| Runner's taper | Your final week |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Ease off the heavy training | Sharpen, don't cram |
| Short, sharp tune-ups | Flashcard sprints |
| Rest before race day | Light day before, good sleep |
| Trust the training | Trust your practice scores |

Runners don't train hardest the day before a race. They rest so they can perform. You do the same.

## 9. Hands-on lab

**Goal:** write your own dated 7-day plan and complete the logistics check.
**Why:** a plan on a real calendar is one you will actually follow.

**You will need**
- A calendar, your exam date, your weak-domain logs, and the logistics checklist. ~25 min.

⚠️ **Before you start:** use your real exam date. If it's not booked yet, pick a target date.

### 9a. Step-by-step instructions

1. Write your exam date on a calendar. Count back seven days.
2. Label each day with its job from Section 3 (Diagnose, Drill, and so on).
3. Fill in your two weakest domains on the Day 6 and Day 5 slots.
4. On the Day 2 slot, list your logistics items to confirm.
5. On Day 1, write "light review + sleep" — and mean it.
6. On Exam Day, write your three-line game plan (brain dump, flag PBQs, no blanks).

### 9b. Expected results

- A dated, seven-day plan you can follow, plus a completed logistics checklist.

- ✅ **You did it if:** every day has one clear job and your logistics are confirmed.

> ⚠️ Exam-day check-in steps and allowed items vary. **[VERIFY WITH CompTIA]**

### 9c. Verify it worked

1. Does each of the seven days have exactly one job?
2. Is your Day 1 truly light (rest, not cram)?

### 9d. Reset / roll back

Nothing to undo. If your date moves, slide the whole plan to match.

> 🔧 **Home-lab continuity:** keep this plan visible until exam day, and check off each day as you go.

## 10. Common mistakes

| ⚠️ Mistake | What happens | How to fix / avoid it |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------------|
| Cramming the day before | You arrive tired and foggy | Day 1 is light review + sleep |
| Skipping the logistics check | You scramble on exam day | Confirm ID, voucher, location/setup on Day 2 |
| Re-reading whole modules | Time lost, gaps remain | Drill only your weak domains |
| Ignoring your practice scores | Needless doubt or delay | Trust two solid scores — go |

## 11. Troubleshooting

| The worry | What to do |
|-----------|------------|
| "I still have weak domains." | Spend Day 3 closing the top one; guess confidently on the rest |
| "I'm too nervous to sleep." | Light review only, then a wind-down routine; rest matters most |
| "My scores aren't at 80% yet." | Consider a later date; keep drilling weak domains first |

# [Module 15 · Lesson 04] Full-Length Practice Exam #1 + Review

> **Unit:** Unit 12 — Capstone & Exam Readiness · **Time:** ~120 min (90 to test + 30 to review) · **Equipment:** E1
> **You'll need first:** Lessons 15-02 (format) and 15-03 (PBQs).

You have learned the whole course. Now let's measure it. A timed practice exam does two jobs. It builds your speed and calm under the clock. And it shows you exactly which topics still need work. This lesson frames how to take the exam, how to score it, and — the most important part — how to review your misses.

---

## 1. Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

- [ ] Take a timed 90-question practice exam under real conditions.
- [ ] Score it and turn the number into a readiness read.
- [ ] Review every missed question and find your weak domains.
- [ ] Log those weak spots for the 15-06 review.

---

## 2. Introduction — why this matters

Reading a chapter tells you what you *recognize*. A timed exam tells you what you can *recall and apply* under pressure. Those are very different. Many people feel ready, then freeze on the clock. Practice fixes that.

A practice exam is also a map. Every missed question points at a topic to review. Skip the review and you waste the exam. Do the review well and each miss becomes a fixed weak spot.

This is the safest place to fail. A wrong answer here costs nothing and teaches a lot.

🎯 **Exam tip:** Treat this like the real thing — 90 minutes, no notes, no phone. The realism is the point.

🔧 **Lab link:** The scenario questions mirror your capstone lab and troubleshooting practice.

---

## 3. Simple explanation

There are three phases: **Take**, **Score**, and **Review**. Do them in order.

### Phase 1 — Take (90 minutes)

- Open `assessments/practice-exams/practice-exam-01.md`.
- Set a 90-minute timer. No notes. No phone.
- Flag hard questions and return. Never leave a blank.
- Answer PBQ-style items fully — every sub-part.

### Phase 2 — Score

- Use `instructor/answer-keys/practice-exam-01-answers.md`.
- Count correct out of 90. Percentage = correct ÷ 90.
- Read your band: 80%+ strong; 70–79% close; under 70% needs review.

### Phase 3 — Review (the important part)

- Read every missed question and the "why."
- Write down the domain of each miss (1–5).
- Tally which domain has the most misses — that is your weak spot.
- Log the weak domains for **Lesson 15-06**.

> **The big idea:** The score is not the point — the review is. Take it under real conditions, then turn every miss into a topic you fix before exam day.

💡 **Tip:** Don't peek at the key until the timer ends. Real conditions give a real read.

⚠️ **Watch out:** A high score with no review still leaves weak spots hidden. Always review.

## 4. Real-world analogy

> **"A practice exam is a dress rehearsal, not opening night."**

| Dress rehearsal | Practice exam |
|-----------------|---------------|
| Full run, real timing | 90 questions, 90 minutes |
| Mistakes are safe | Wrong answers cost nothing |
| Notes what to fix | Shows your weak domains |
| You rehearse again | You retake with #2 (15-05) |

A rehearsal is where you find the rough spots — on purpose — so opening night is smooth.

## 9. Hands-on lab

**Goal:** take Practice Exam #1 under real conditions, then review it into a weak-domain plan.
**Why:** the review turns a score into a study plan you can act on.

**You will need**
- The exam file, the answer key, a 90-minute timer, and the weak-domain tracker. ~120 minutes total.

⚠️ **Before you start:** no notes, no phone, no key until the timer ends. Realism gives a real read.

### 9a. Step-by-step instructions

1. Open `assessments/practice-exams/practice-exam-01.md`.
2. Set a 90-minute timer. Begin.
3. Flag hard questions; return to them. Leave no blanks.
4. When time ends, stop and open the answer key.
5. Score: count correct out of 90; compute the percentage.
6. For every miss, write the question number and its domain (1–5) on `wb-15-04-hands-on-observation.md`.
7. Tally misses per domain. Circle your top one or two weak domains.

### 9b. Expected results

- A completed 90-question exam, a score, and a per-domain tally of misses.

- ✅ **You did it if:** you finished in time, left no blanks, and named your weakest domain(s).

> ⚠️ Any command output in a question is a varying example. **[TO VERIFY ON HARDWARE]** Format specifics may change — **[VERIFY WITH CompTIA]**.

### 9c. Verify it worked

1. Did you take it under real conditions (timed, no notes)?
2. Do you have a clear list of weak domains to review?

### 9d. Reset / roll back

Nothing to undo. Keep your tally — you'll compare it after Exam #2 (15-05).

> 🔧 **Home-lab continuity:** carry your weak-domain list into Lessons 15-05 and 15-06.

## 10. Common mistakes

| ⚠️ Mistake | What happens | How to fix / avoid it |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------------|
| Peeking at the key early | You lose a real read | Wait for the timer to end |
| Scoring but not reviewing | Weak spots stay hidden | Review every miss by domain |
| Using notes | The result is inflated | Simulate real, closed-book conditions |
| Leaving blanks | Lost easy points | Guess — never leave a blank |

---

## 11. Troubleshooting

| The worry | What to do |
|-----------|------------|
| "I ran out of time." | Practice pacing; flag and return faster |
| "I scored low." | Good — now you know where to study; review by domain |
| "Same domain keeps failing." | That is your #1 review target for 15-06 |
| "I'm not improving." | Space your retakes; review misses before retaking |

**Safety:** don't shine bright light into anyone's eyes.

---

### Step 1 — Make a tube (5 pts)
Roll paper into a tube (or use a paper-towel tube). It stands in for the core + cladding.

### Step 2 — Send light through (5 pts)
In a dim room, shine the light in one end. Does it come out the other end? ☐ Yes ☐ No

### Step 3 — Bend it (5 pts)
Bend the tube gently — light still exits. Bend it sharply — the light dims.

Gentle bend: light ☐ still exits &nbsp; Sharp bend: light ☐ dims/blocked
Lesson: never ____________ a real fiber sharply.

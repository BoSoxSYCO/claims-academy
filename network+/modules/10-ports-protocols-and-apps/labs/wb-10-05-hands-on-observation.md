**Goal:** build a 16-card port deck and run one active-recall session.

**You will need:** 16 index cards (or a flashcard app) and a pen.

### Steps

**1.** Make 16 cards. Front = a **port**; back = **protocol + job + TCP/UDP**. Cards made? ☐ (2 pts)

**2.** Shuffle. Round 1 (number → protocol): say the back from memory, then flip.

**3.** Record Round 1 score: **______ / 16** correct.  (4 pts for an honest count)

**4.** Round 2 (protocol → number): flip the deck, go the other way. Score: **______ / 16.**  (4 pts)

## The one idea behind the whole course

Learn this handful of "this = that" pairings once, and every later stop clicks into place.

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="🛣️ Road = Cable" >The path a message travels down.</Card>
  <Card title="🚦 Intersection = Switch">Sends each message down only the right road.</Card>
  <Card title="🏠 House = Device">Your laptop, phone, or printer.</Card>
  <Card title="🚗 Car = Packet">One chunk of data, making a trip.</Card>
  <Card title="🔖 License plate = MAC address">A device's permanent hardware name.</Card>
  <Card title="🛂 On-ramp = Router">The way out to other neighborhoods and the internet.</Card>
</CardGrid>

## How to ride

The course is **8 lines** and 96 **stops**. Start at the first stop and follow the line, or jump straight to the switch demo to see how hands-on it gets. Your place is saved as you go.

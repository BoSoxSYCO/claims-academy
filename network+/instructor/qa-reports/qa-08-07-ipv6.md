> **Accuracy note:** Compression examples verified: `2001:0db8:0000:0000:0000:ff00:0042:8329` → `2001:db8::ff00:42:8329`; `fe80:…:0001` → `fe80::1`; worksheet `…:00a3:0000:1234` → `2001:db8::a3:0:1234`. Prefixes are standard: `::1` loopback, `::` unspecified, `fe80::/10` link-local, `2000::/3` global unicast, `ff00::/8` multicast (no broadcast). `2001:db8::/32` is the reserved documentation range (RFC 3849) — safe to print. Graded items each have a single longest zero run, so beginners aren't tested on the equal-length tie-break (noted in teaching notes). `ipconfig` used read-only (Prime Directive #7).

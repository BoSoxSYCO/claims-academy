# Teaching Notes — NA-M00-L05 "Setting Up Your Workspace and a Lab Notebook"

> 🔒 Instructor material. **Module:** 00 · **Unit:** 1 · **Objective:** orientation · **Time:** ~25 min

---

## Learning objective (one line)

Learners set up a safe workspace and a lab notebook they'll use for the whole course.

## Why this lesson matters

This lesson turns the on-ramp into a launchpad. A tidy, safe workspace prevents mistakes and protects the gear (some of it shared). A lab notebook builds the documentation habit that runs through every later lab and appears on the exam. Doing this once, well, makes all 15 later modules' hands-on work smoother. It's the final on-ramp lesson and hands off directly to Module 01.

## The three parts

1. **Workspace** — near power, room to spread, good light, tidy cables.
2. **Safety** — never force a connector, power down to move cables, drinks away, and protect the shared switch (change only what you were told to).
3. **Lab notebook** — a simple form of documentation: date, what you did, what you saw. Same notebook all course; ties to the note-taking habit from 00-03.

Plus a **pre-lab checklist**: outlet, space, notebook open, which lab.

## Common misconceptions (and fixes)

- **"Force the connector."** No — it bends the RJ45 pins; line it up and it clicks.
- **"The notebook is optional."** No — it's the record that saves hours and builds the exam-tested habit.
- **"The shared switch is fine to change."** No — a wrong change can lock others out.

## Pacing guidance

- ~25 min. The §9 setup is the core and should leave a real, reusable workspace and notebook.
- Where learners hesitate: not having a dedicated space. Reassure that a cleared table corner works.

## How CompTIA tests it

Documentation and safe-change practice both appear on the exam. Starting the notebook and the shared-switch caution here plants those habits early.

## Accuracy note (Prime Directive #7)

All advice is real and safe. The shared-switch caution is per CLAUDE.md. Any rule specific to the learner's exact shared unit is marked `[TO VERIFY ON HARDWARE]`. No invented product specs. No new glossary terms — documentation, link light, RJ45, console port, and switch were all taught earlier and are reused (grepped first); "lab notebook" and "workspace" are plain words, not glossarized.

## Course close

This is the final lesson of Module 00 and the last teaching lesson of the whole NetworkAcademy+ curriculum. With it published, all 16 modules (00–15) are complete. Congratulate learners on finishing the on-ramp and send them into Module 01 — Computer Foundations.

## Extension ideas (fast learners)

- Create a reusable pre-lab checklist card to keep at the workspace.
- Set up a simple digital notebook template with the three columns.

## Support ideas (struggling learners)

- Help them pick and clear a small dedicated spot.
- Start the notebook together with the first entry.
- Anchor one safety rule at a time; "never force a connector" is the first.

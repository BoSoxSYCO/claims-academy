# Teaching Notes — NA-M15-L05 "Full-Length Practice Exam #2 + Review"

> 🔒 Instructor material. **Module:** 15 · **Unit:** 12 · **Objective:** exam-readiness · **Time:** ~120 min

---

## Learning objective (one line)

Learners take a second timed exam and compare it to the first to judge readiness.

## Why this lesson matters

One score is a snapshot and can mislead — a good day, a lucky guess. Two scores across two different forms show a trend, which is far harder to fake. This lesson turns "I took a test" into "I can see my progress and know where I still lag." The comparison is the graded skill.

## The three phases

1. **Take** — Exam #2 (`practice-exam-02.md`), 90 minutes, real conditions, no blanks.
2. **Score** — correct ÷ 90.
3. **Compare** — Exam #2 vs Exam #1, overall and per domain; read the ready signal.

## The ready signal (teach it clearly)

- **Ready:** 80%+ on both exams, no domain badly behind.
- **Almost:** one exam lower — drill the lagging domain.
- **Not yet:** either exam under 70% — re-study the weak module first.

## Exam #2 itself

`assessments/practice-exams/practice-exam-02.md` — a genuine second form: 90 new questions with different wording/scenarios, same five-domain coverage and tags, difficulty ladder, PBQ-style/scenario, true/false-with-why, and matching. Every item is answerable from taught content (Modules 01–14). Full key: `instructor/answer-keys/practice-exam-02-answers.md`.

## Common misconceptions (and fixes)

- **"One score is enough."** No — the trend across two is the real read.
- **"Retake Exam #1 from memory."** No — that inflates the score; use the second form.
- **"Only overall matters."** No — a single lagging domain can sink the real exam.

## Pacing guidance

- ~120 min (90 test + 30 compare). Space #1 and #2 a day or two apart.
- Where learners stall: comparing per domain (not just overall) and being honest about a lagging area.

## How CompTIA tends to test it

Exam #2 mirrors the real exam's mixed-domain, laddered format with fresh wording — good preparation for seeing familiar content phrased in new ways.

## Accuracy note (Prime Directive #7)

All 90 questions answerable from taught content. Sample outputs are varying examples `[TO VERIFY ON HARDWARE]`; exam-policy specifics `[VERIFY WITH CompTIA]`. The 90/90/720-of-900 facts match Lesson 15-02.

## Extension ideas (fast learners)

- Plot a small bar chart of both exams by domain to visualize the trend.
- Rewrite every remaining miss in their own words.

## Support ideas (struggling learners)

- Compare one domain together as a class before the full comparison.
- If a score dropped, review misses calmly — #2 may simply have felt harder.
- Anchor the rule: "two scores, per domain, then the ready signal."

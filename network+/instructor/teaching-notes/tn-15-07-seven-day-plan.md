# Teaching Notes — NA-M15-L07 "Your 7-Day Pre-Exam Plan"

> 🔒 Instructor material. **Module:** 15 · **Unit:** 12 · **Objective:** exam-readiness · **Time:** ~30 min

---

## Learning objective (one line)

Learners execute a calm, structured final week using their Module 15 tools.

## Why this lesson matters

The week before the exam decides how much of the preparation actually shows up. This lesson removes guesswork and panic: a day-by-day plan that paces review, practice exams, logistics, and rest. It ties together every Module 15 method — the two practice exams (15-04/15-05), the compare method (15-05), PBQ practice (15-03), the weak-spot sprint and flashcards (15-06), and the exam-day game plan (15-02) — into one runnable schedule.

## The 7-day taper (one job per day)

- **Day 7** Diagnose — Exam #1, list weak domains.
- **Day 6** Drill weakest domain (flashcards, active recall, spaced).
- **Day 5** Drill next domain + spaced repeats.
- **Day 4** Re-measure — Exam #2, compare per domain.
- **Day 3** Close gaps + PBQ practice.
- **Day 2** Light full pass + logistics check.
- **Day 1** Rest, prep, sleep (light review only).
- **Exam Day** Execute — brain dump, flag PBQs, bank fast MC, no blanks.

## Common misconceptions (and fixes)

- **"Cram the day before."** No — Day 1 is light + sleep; a rested brain wins.
- **"Logistics can wait."** No — confirm ID/voucher/location on Day 2.
- **"Nerves mean I should delay."** No — trust two solid practice scores.

## Pacing guidance

- ~30 min to build the dated plan in class; the plan itself runs across the real week.
- Have learners write real dates on a calendar — a visible plan gets followed.
- Where learners stall: keeping Day 1 truly light, and not skipping logistics.

## How CompTIA tends to test it

This is meta — no item asks about a study plan. But a paced, rested candidate performs better on every real question and every PBQ.

## Extension ideas (fast learners)

- Build a two-week version if the exam is farther out (repeat the drill/re-measure cycle).
- Add a mock "exam morning" run-through of the game plan.

## Support ideas (struggling learners)

- Fill in the dated calendar together, day by day.
- If scores aren't at 80% yet, advise moving the date and extending the drill days.
- Anchor the message: "sharpen, don't cram — and trust your scores."

# Teaching Notes — NA-M04-L01 "Why Networks Exist (The Problem They Solve)"

> 🔒 Instructor materials. Pairs with the lesson and `wb-04-01-answers.md`.

## Objective in one line
Learners can explain why networks exist: to share resources and communicate.

## Where this sits
First lesson of Module 04 (Networking Fundamentals) and the **opener of Unit 2 (real networking)**. It supplies the "why" that motivates every later technical topic. Time: ~20 min + ~40 min worksheets/lab.

## The anchor
The **"town with roads and phone lines"** analogy and **sneakernet** (carrying files by hand) as the pain networks remove.

## Common misconceptions (and the fix)
- "A network = the internet." → Fix: the internet is one huge network; small home/office networks exist everywhere.
- "You need many computers." → Fix: two connected devices are a network.
- Losing the "why." → Fix: every topic serves sharing or communicating.

## Pacing tips
- Keep it concrete and relatable — brainstorm the resources learners already share (Wi-Fi, printer, streaming).
- This is a light, motivating lesson; no deep tech yet (topologies/models come next).

## How CompTIA tests it
The entire exam serves this goal. Framing it now improves retention of later details.

## Support ideas
- List every networked device in the learner's home and what each one shares.

## Extension ideas
- Discuss the difference between a small local network and the global internet.

# Teaching Notes — NA-M07-L04 "Access Points, Firewalls, and Other Gear"

> 🔒 Instructor materials. Pairs with the lesson and `wb-07-04-answers.md`.

## Objective in one line
Learners identify common network devices (AP, modem, firewall, load balancer, proxy, IDS/IPS, PoE) and match each to its one-word job.

## Where this sits
Fourth lesson of Module 07 — a device survey after hub/switch/router (07-01). Sets up the Dell switch (07-05). Several devices hide in the learner's home box. Time: ~28 min + ~40 min worksheets/lab.

## The anchor
**A building:** a guard at the gate (firewall), a door to the street (modem), a guest Wi-Fi desk (AP), extra checkout lanes (load balancer), a mail-forwarding office (proxy), a camera+alarm (IDS/IPS), power in the walls (PoE).

## Common misconceptions (and the fix)
- "Modem = router." → Fix: modem talks to the ISP; router connects networks.
- "AP = router." → Fix: an AP only bridges Wi-Fi to the wired network.
- "IDS = IPS." → Fix: IDS alerts; IPS blocks.
- "A firewall speeds things up." → Fix: it filters for safety, not speed.

## Pacing tips
- The find-your-gear lab (7.4.E) is the payoff — many devices live in the home box.
- Drill one-word jobs: Wi-Fi / ISP door / filter / spread / middleman / watch / power.

## How CompTIA tests it
Device-to-job identification; IDS vs. IPS; modem vs. router; PoE purpose.

## Support ideas
- Re-use the building analogy for each device a learner raises.

## Extension ideas
- NAS, wireless LAN controllers, next-gen firewalls, and VPN concentrators for fast learners.

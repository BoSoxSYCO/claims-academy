# Teaching Notes — NA-M15-L02 "Exam-Day Logistics and What to Expect"

> 🔒 Instructor material. **Module:** 15 · **Unit:** 12 · **Objective:** exam-readiness (meta) · **Time:** ~30 min

---

## Learning objective (one line)

Learners know how the N10-009 exam is scored and timed, and how to work it strategically.

## Why this lesson matters

Content knowledge is only half the battle. A learner who knows networking cold can still lose points to the clock, to panic, or to a scary early PBQ. This lesson removes surprises: format, timing, scoring, and a simple time strategy. Calm, prepared candidates score higher.

## The confident facts (teach plainly)

- **90 minutes.** **720 of 900** to pass — a **scaled score**, not a percent.
- **Up to ~90 questions**, a mix of multiple-choice and **PBQs** (hands-on tasks).
- **No penalty for guessing** — never leave a blank.
- Delivered at a **Pearson VUE** center or via **online proctoring**; bring a valid government photo ID.

## What to mark [VERIFY WITH CompTIA]

Exact question counts vary by form; retake/waiting-period rules and current voucher price change over time. The lesson marks these rather than stating them as fixed facts (Prime Directive #7 applies to exam facts too).

## The strategy (drill it)

Flag an early PBQ → bank the fast multiple-choice → return to flagged PBQs → guess any blanks → review → submit. The buffet analogy (§4) makes it stick: easy dishes first, circle back, never leave with an empty plate.

## Common misconceptions (and fixes)

- **"720 is 72%."** No — it is a scaled score; questions are weighted and converted.
- **"Do PBQs in order."** No — flag and return; they eat time.
- **"A blank is safer than a wrong guess."** No — guessing has no penalty; a blank is a sure zero.

## Pacing guidance

- ~30 min. This is a meta lesson — light on new networking, heavy on habits.
- The §9 timed dry run is the core activity; have learners actually run the clock.
- Where learners stall: believing 720 is a percent, and fear of PBQs.

## How CompTIA tends to test it

This content is not itself tested — no item asks "how long is the exam." But the strategy directly protects the learner's score on every real question.

## Accuracy note (Prime Directive #7)

Confident facts are stated plainly; everything variable is marked `[VERIFY WITH CompTIA]`. No fabricated specifics (no invented price, no invented exact count).

## Extension ideas (fast learners)

- Write a personal exam-day checklist and a minute-by-minute timing plan.
- Practice the timed dry run until they finish with margin every time.

## Support ideas (struggling learners)

- Give the buffet analogy and the five-step strategy as a printed card.
- Walk one PBQ-flag decision together before the solo dry run.
- Anchor two rules: "720 of 900 passes" and "never leave a blank."

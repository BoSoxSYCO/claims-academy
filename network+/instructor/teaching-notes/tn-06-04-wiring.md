# Teaching Notes — NA-M06-L04 "Straight-Through vs. Crossover, T568A/B"

> 🔒 Instructor materials. Pairs with the lesson and `wb-06-04-answers.md`.

## Objective in one line
Learners tell straight-through from crossover, know T568A/B swap orange/green, and match cable type to device types (with Auto-MDI-X context).

## Where this sits
Fourth lesson of Module 06. Builds on the RJ45 (06-02) and twisted-pair (06-01). Feeds Ethernet standards (06-05) and the home-lab cabling. Time: ~28 min + ~40 min worksheets/lab.

## The anchor
**Mouth to ear.** Send must reach the other's listen. Unlike devices line up naturally (straight-through); like devices need the pairs crossed (crossover). A translator (Auto-MDI-X) makes crossing automatic.

## Common misconceptions (and the fix)
- "A and B carry data differently." → Fix: both work; just be consistent end to end.
- "Two PCs always need a crossover." → Fix: only on old gear; Auto-MDI-X handles it now.
- "Reading with tabs up." → Fix: hold both ends tab-down, pins toward you.
- "Mixing A/B is fine for straight-through." → Fix: that makes an accidental crossover.

## Pacing tips
- The compare-both-ends lab (6.4.E) is the payoff — everyone reads a real cable.
- Emphasize: today straight-through is the everyday cable; crossover is exam + old gear.

## How CompTIA tests it
Straight-through vs. crossover use-cases; the T568A/B orange-green swap; Auto-MDI-X's effect.

## Support ideas
- Physically hold two ends tab-down and point at the orange/green pairs.

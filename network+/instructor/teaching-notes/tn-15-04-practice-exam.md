# Teaching Notes — NA-M15-L04 "Full-Length Practice Exam #1 + Review"

> 🔒 Instructor material. **Module:** 15 · **Unit:** 12 · **Objective:** exam-readiness · **Time:** ~120 min

---

## Learning objective (one line)

Learners take a timed 90-question exam and convert it into a weak-domain study plan.

## Why this lesson matters

This is the first full dress rehearsal. It measures recall and pacing under real pressure — very different from recognizing content in a chapter. Just as important, it turns a score into a map: every miss points at a topic to fix. The graded skill here is the **review**, not the number.

## The three phases

1. **Take** — 90 minutes, no notes, no phone, no blanks. Flag and return.
2. **Score** — correct ÷ 90; read the band (80%+ / 70–79% / under 70%).
3. **Review** — read every miss, tag its domain, tally, log weak domains for 15-06.

## The exam itself

`assessments/practice-exams/practice-exam-01.md` — 90 questions across all five domains, tagged with objective + source module, difficulty ladder, with PBQ-style/scenario, true/false-with-why, and matching items. Every question is answerable from taught content (Modules 01–14). Full key with per-question "why" is `instructor/answer-keys/practice-exam-01-answers.md`.

## Common misconceptions (and fixes)

- **"The score is the point."** No — the review is; the score just aims it.
- **"Review is optional if I passed."** No — a high score can hide weak spots and lucky guesses.
- **"Notes are fine for practice."** No — real conditions give a real read.

## Pacing guidance

- ~120 min total: 90 to take, ~30 to score and review.
- If class time is short, take it as homework and use class for the review.
- Where learners stall: pacing (running long) and skipping the per-domain tally.

## How CompTIA tends to test it

The exam mirrors the real format: mixed domains in one sitting, PBQ-style items early, a difficulty ladder, and plausible distractors drawn from real beginner mistakes.

## Accuracy note (Prime Directive #7)

Every question is answerable from already-taught material — nothing new is introduced. Sample command outputs are varying examples `[TO VERIFY ON HARDWARE]`; exam-policy specifics are marked `[VERIFY WITH CompTIA]`. The 90/90/720-of-900 facts match Lesson 15-02.

## Extension ideas (fast learners)

- Graph per-domain scores across Exams #1 and #2 to see improvement.
- Rewrite each missed question in their own words to lock it in.

## Support ideas (struggling learners)

- Take it in two halves (45 questions each) before a full timed run.
- Review one domain's misses together as a class.
- Anchor the habit: "score, then review every miss by domain."

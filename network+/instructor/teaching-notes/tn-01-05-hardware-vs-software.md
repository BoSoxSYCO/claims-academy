# Teaching Notes — NA-M01-L05 "Hardware vs. Software"

> 🔒 Instructor materials. Pairs with the lesson and `wb-01-05-answers.md`.

## Objective in one line
Learners can sort hardware vs. software and understand a switch is both (hardware + firmware).

## Where this sits
Fifth content lesson. Sharpens a distinction touched in 01-01/01-04. Introduces **firmware** as a bridge to network gear. Time: ~20 min (shorter) + ~40 min worksheets/lab.

## The anchor
The **music-player analogy** (device = hardware, songs = software) and the **touch test** ("Can I touch it?").

## Common misconceptions (and the fix)
- Apps called "hardware." → Touch test.
- The drive vs. its contents. → The drive is hardware; the programs on it are software.
- "Is Windows hardware?" → No — it's the OS, which is software.

## Pacing tips
- Keep it brisk; this is a clarifying lesson, not a heavy one.
- Introduce firmware lightly — just "software built into a device like your switch." Don't go into flashing/updates yet.

## How CompTIA tests it
The hardware-vs-software split underlies troubleshooting: is a fault physical (hardware) or configuration/instructions (software)? Model that split in the Q10 scenario.

## Support ideas
- Rapid-fire: hold up or name objects; learners shout "hardware!" or "software!"

## Extension ideas
- Classify parts of the switch and router as hardware, firmware, or configuration.

# Teaching Notes — NA-M00-L02 "How This Academy Works"

> 🔒 Instructor material. **Module:** 00 · **Unit:** 1 · **Objective:** orientation · **Time:** ~20 min

---

## Learning objective (one line)

Learners know how the course is organized and how to work through one lesson.

## Why this lesson matters

A learner who understands the layout wastes no energy on "what do I do next?" and spends it all on the content. This lesson makes the course's repeatable pattern explicit: the same seven-part lesson shape, the same practice parts, and one place to look up any word. Predictability lowers anxiety and raises completion.

## The parts (describe only what exists)

- **Structure:** units → modules (00–15) → lessons.
- **Lesson shape:** the seven-part spine (why → key words → idea+diagram → see it → steps → check → remember).
- **Per lesson:** a workbook (worksheets), a quiz (answers in a separate key), a cheat sheet.
- **Shared:** one alphabetical glossary, two full practice exams, a flashcard deck, and labs for hands-on work.

## The one-lesson routine (drill it)

Read → look at the diagram → do the workbook → take the quiz (then check the key) → keep the cheat sheet handy. Simple enough to remember, complete enough to work.

## Common misconceptions (and fixes)

- **"Skipping around is fine."** No — lessons build in order.
- **"The workbook is optional."** Practice is where learning sticks.
- **"Answers are in the quiz."** No — they're in the instructor key, on purpose.

## Pacing guidance

- ~20 min. Keep it a relaxed tour, not a memory test.
- The best activity is the §9 folder tour — it makes the structure concrete.
- Where learners hesitate: technical-looking folder names. Reassure that comfort comes fast.

## How CompTIA tends to test it

CompTIA doesn't test course structure. But learners who actually use the workbooks, quizzes, and flashcards perform better on every objective.

## Accuracy note (Prime Directive #7)

Describe only what exists in this repo: lessons, workbooks, quizzes, answer keys, cheat sheets, one glossary, labs, two practice exams, and the core flashcard deck. Do **not** promise videos, a login/account system, auto-grading, or progress tracking — none of those exist here. No new glossary terms (course-structure words are not networking terms).

## Extension ideas (fast learners)

- Bookmark the glossary and one cheat sheet for fast access.
- Write their own one-line summary of the lesson routine.

## Support ideas (struggling learners)

- Walk the folder tour together, naming each part aloud.
- Post the five-step routine somewhere visible.
- Reassure: "Learn the pattern once; it never changes on you."

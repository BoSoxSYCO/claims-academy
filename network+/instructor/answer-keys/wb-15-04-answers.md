## Quiz answers (`quiz-15-04-knowledge-check.md`)

1. **B — 90 minutes.** Match the real exam's time to build pacing.

2. **B — only after the timer ends.** Real conditions give a real read.

3. **C — reviewing every miss.** The review turns a score into a study plan.

4. **B — domain (1–5).** Tag each miss so you can find weak domains.

5. **False.** A high score can still hide weak spots; always review every miss so a lucky guess doesn't mask a gap.

6. **B — carry it into Lesson 15-06.** The weak-spot review uses it.

7. **B — to get a true read of readiness.** Notes or extra time inflate the result.

8. **Matching:** Take → C (a completed exam); Score → A (a percentage); Review → B (a weak-domain list).

9. **90.** Score = correct ÷ 90.

10. **Scenario —** Review your Security misses and their "why," re-study Module 13 (the security module), then retake — targeting Domain 4 — with Practice Exam #2. *(Accept any answer that reviews the weak domain's module before retaking.)*

### Short answer (`wb-15-04-short-answer.md`) — model answers
1. To get a true read of recall and pacing under pressure, not just recognition.
2. You must review every miss — scoring alone leaves the weak topics hidden.
3. Read its "why," note its domain, and re-study that module.
4. It shows which domain(s) are weakest, so you know where to focus.
5. 80%+ strong; 70–79% close; under 70% review before the next attempt.
6. Because peeking removes the real, timed read of your readiness.
7. Your weak-domain list — Lesson 15-06 is the weak-spot review built around it.

### Vocabulary review (`wb-15-04-vocabulary-review.md`)
- **Scaled score** — a converted exam score (100–900); 720 passes.
- **Performance-Based Question (PBQ)** — a hands-on exam task; answer every sub-part.
- **Domain** — one of the five N10-009 topic areas.
- **Baseline** — your starting score to improve on.

**Part 2 (five domains):** 1 Networking Concepts · 2 Network Implementation · 3 Network Operations · 4 Network Security · 5 Network Troubleshooting.
**Part 3 (three phases):** Take · Score · Review.

### Hands-on observation (`wb-15-04-hands-on-observation.md`)
Learner's own score and per-domain tally. Check that total correct + total misses = 90, the readiness band is circled, and at least one weak domain is named for 15-06.

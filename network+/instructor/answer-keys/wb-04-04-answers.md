## Quiz 4.4.Q — Knowledge Check (10 pts)

1. **B** — A topology is the shape of a network (how nodes and links are arranged).
2. **B** — In a star, every node connects to one central device (switch/router).
3. **C** — A bus uses one shared backbone cable.
4. **A** — A ring connects each node to two neighbors in a closed loop.
5. **D** — A mesh has many paths; it is the most reliable but the most expensive.
6. **False.** Good answer: in a star, one broken cable drops only that one node; the rest keep working.
7. **B** — A typical home network is a star, centered on the router.
8. Star → **C**, Bus → **A**, Mesh → **B**.
9. **center** (the central device — switch/router).
10. **Star.** Why: one cable fault affects only that one computer, and adding a PC is just one more link to the center.

**Scoring:** 1 pt each. Pass = 8/10 (80%).

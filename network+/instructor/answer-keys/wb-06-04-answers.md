## Quiz 6.4.Q — Knowledge Check (10 pts)

1. **B** — A pinout is the order the 8 wires go into the RJ45 pins.
2. **B** — T568A and T568B swap the orange and green pairs.
3. **B** — Straight-through uses the same standard on both ends.
4. **B** — Crossover uses T568A on one end and T568B on the other.
5. **B** — Straight-through connects unlike devices (PC to switch).
6. **True.** Good answer: Auto-MDI-X detects and fixes the crossover internally, so a straight-through cable links almost anything.
7. **B** — Traditionally, two PCs connect directly with a crossover.
8. Straight-through → **B**, Crossover → **A**, Auto-MDI-X → **C**.
9. **orange** (orange and green pairs swap).
10. A **crossover** cable; it connects **like** devices (e.g., PC to PC, switch to switch on old gear).

## Worksheet 6.4.D — Short answer (20 pts)

1. They swap the orange and green pairs; otherwise the same. Both work if used consistently. *(idea /3, why /2)*
2. Straight-through = same standard both ends; crossover = T568A one end, T568B the other. *(idea /3, why /2)*
3. Straight-through = unlike devices (PC↔switch); crossover = like devices (PC↔PC). *(idea /3, why /2)*
4. Auto-MDI-X auto-detects and fixes the crossover inside the port, so a straight-through works nearly everywhere. *(idea /3, why /2)*

## Quiz answers (`quiz-00-02-knowledge-check.md`)

1. **A — modules, numbered 00 to 15.** The course is organized into numbered modules. *(Not one file, random pages, or videos.)*

2. **B — in order, one at a time.** Each lesson builds on the ones before it.

3. **B — in a separate instructor answer key.** Answers are kept out of the quiz so you think first.

4. **A — defining every term, listed alphabetically.** The glossary is your term look-up.

5. **B — a one-page printable summary.** A cheat sheet condenses a topic for quick review.

6. **True.** Every lesson uses the same seven-part shape, so you always know what comes next.

7. **B — look it up in the glossary.** Every term is defined there; clear up fuzzy words right away.

8. **Matching:** Workbook → B (practice worksheets); Quiz → A (knowledge check); Cheat sheet → C (one-page summary).

9. **practice** — two full practice exams, plus flashcards.

10. **Scenario —** Read the lesson top to bottom; look at the diagram until it makes sense; do the workbook worksheets; take the quiz and check the answer key; keep the cheat sheet handy (and look up any fuzzy word in the glossary). *(Accept any answer with read → look → workbook → quiz → cheat sheet.)*

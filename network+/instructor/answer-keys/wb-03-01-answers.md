## Worksheet 3.1.C — Switches to bits (20 pts)

Bit values: 1. 1  2. 0  3. 1  4. 1  5. 0  6. 0  7. 1  8. 0.
- 8 switches (bits); a group of 8 bits = a **byte**; pattern = `10110010`.
**Why (+2):** a switch is either fully on or fully off (electricity flowing or not) — there is no in-between state.

## Worksheet 4.1.D — Short answer (20 pts)

1. A network connects devices so they can share resources and communicate; we build them to avoid isolation. *(idea /3, why /2)*
2. Roads/phone lines connect isolated houses so they can share and talk — like cables/Wi-Fi connect computers. *(idea /3, clarity /2)*
3. Sneakernet = carrying files on a USB by hand; a network shares instantly, with no walking. *(idea /3, why /2)*
4. Connect all five PCs and the printer to the network so any PC can print to the one shared printer. *(idea /3, why /2)*

### Short answer (`wb-15-05-short-answer.md`) — model answers
1. A single score can be a fluke; two scores show a trend that is harder to fake.
2. Reusing #1 from memory inflates the score and hides real gaps; a fresh form gives an honest read.
3. Each domain's misses, so you can spot a single area that still lags behind the overall score.
4. 80%+ on both exams, with no domain badly behind.
5. Your review is working — keep the same study pattern.
6. Back-to-back testing adds fatigue that skews the second score; spacing keeps it honest.
7. Re-study its module and log it for the Lesson 15-06 weak-spot review.

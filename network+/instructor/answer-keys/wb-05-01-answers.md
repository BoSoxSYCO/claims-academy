## Quiz 5.1.Q — Knowledge Check (10 pts)

1. **B** — A model is a simplified plan that shows just the parts you need.
2. **A** — A layer is one job in the model, stacked on the others.
3. **B** — Layered models let you troubleshoot by asking "which layer?"
4. **C** — The OSI model has 7 layers.
5. **A** — The TCP/IP model has 4 layers.
6. **False.** Good answer: they don't compete — both describe the same networking, just split into different numbers of layers.
7. **A** — A department stands for a layer (each does one job and hands off).
8. Model → **C**, Layer → **A**, Stack → **B**.
9. **down** (data moves down the stack to be sent).
10. Finding the layer narrows the search: you can tell a cable/signal problem (low layer) from an app problem (high layer) instead of checking everything at once.

**Scoring:** 1 pt each. Pass = 8/10 (80%).

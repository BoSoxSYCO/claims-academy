## Worksheet 1.3.D — Short answer (20 pts)

1. Input sends info in (e.g., keyboard); output sends info out (e.g., screen). *(idea /3, why /2)*
2. A port is a shaped slot for a device/cable; different shapes so only the right plug fits. *(idea /3, why /2)*
3. The Ethernet port; recognize it as a small near-square slot wider than a charger port. *(idea /3, clarity /2)*
4. The new laptop likely has no built-in Ethernet port; buy a **USB-to-Ethernet adapter**. *(idea /3, why /2)*

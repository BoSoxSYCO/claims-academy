## Quiz answers (`quiz-15-07-knowledge-check.md`)

1. **B — sharpen what you know and close gaps.** The final week is not for new topics. *(Cramming or doing nothing both hurt.)*

2. **B — diagnose — take Practice Exam #1 and list weak domains.** Day 7 makes your map for the week.

3. **A — take Practice Exam #2 and compare to #1.** Day 4 re-measures and confirms weak spots are closing.

4. **B — light review, prep, and good sleep.** Rest beats cramming the night before.

5. **False.** Do the logistics check on Day 2 — exam morning is too late to fix a missing ID or wrong location.

6. **B — do a quick brain dump of facts you fear forgetting.** Get them onto scratch paper first.

7. **B — trust them and go.** Two solid scores mean you're ready; nerves aren't a reason to delay.

8. **Matching:** Day 6 → C (drill weakest domain); Day 4 → A (re-measure with Exam #2); Day 1 → B (rest and prep).

9. **taper.** The final week is a taper, not a last sprint.

10. **Scenario —** Day 7: take/score Exam #1, confirm Security is weakest. Days 6–5: flashcard sprints on Security (Deck 6) with active recall + spaced repeats; drill the exact topics missed. Day 4: take Exam #2, compare Security #1 vs #2. Day 3: close any remaining Security gaps + PBQ practice. Day 2: light full pass + logistics check. Day 1: rest, prep, sleep. *(Accept any plan that diagnoses, drills the weak domain, re-measures, closes gaps, then tapers to rest.)*

### Short answer (`wb-15-07-short-answer.md`) — model answers
1. New topics rarely stick in a week and add stress; sharpening what you know pays off more.
2. Diagnose with Exam #1 to map your weak domains — you need that map before you can target your study.
3. To confirm your weak domains improved and get a second, trend-forming score before booking.
4. A rested brain recalls and reasons better; cramming late adds fatigue and little knowledge.
5. Valid photo ID, exam voucher, test-center location/route (or a tested webcam and quiet room), and confirmed date/time.
6. A quick brain dump, then flag PBQs, bank the fast multiple-choice, and never leave a blank.
7. Trust the two solid scores and go — nerves alone are not a reason to delay.

### Vocabulary review (`wb-15-07-vocabulary-review.md`)

- **Voucher** — the prepaid code that pays for your exam seat.
- **Online proctoring** — taking the exam from home while a remote proctor watches.

# Answer Key — Lesson NA-M06-L06 "Cable Testing and Common Physical Problems"

> 🔒 **Instructor / self-check only.** Covers Quiz 6.6.Q and Worksheets 6.6.A–6.6.G.

**Faults:** open (break) · short (touching) · miswire (wrong order) · split pair (crossed, passes but errors) · attenuation (too long) · interference/EMI (noise). **Tools:** cable tester (opens/shorts/miswires) · tone generator + probe (trace).

---

## Quiz 6.6.Q — Knowledge Check (10 pts)

1. **B** — An open is a broken or cut wire (no continuity).
2. **B** — A short is two wires touching when they shouldn't.
3. **A** — Attenuation is the signal getting weaker over distance.
4. **A** — A cable tester finds opens, shorts, and miswires.
5. **B** — A tone generator and probe traces/finds one cable in a bundle.
6. **False.** Good answer: a link light can appear even with a split pair or other fault; only a cable tester confirms the wiring.
7. **B** — Check the link light and whether the plug is fully seated first.
8. Open → **C**, Short → **A**, Continuity → **B**.
9. **attenuation**.
10. Any correct first-three: (1) link light / seated? (2) re-seat both ends (3) swap a known-good cable.

**Scoring:** 1 pt each. Pass = 8/10 (80%).

## Worksheet 6.6.D — Short answer (20 pts)

1. An open is a broken wire (no path); a short is two wires touching (scrambled signal). *(idea /3, why /2)*
2. A cable tester checks each wire for opens/shorts/miswires; a tone generator sends a tone so a probe can trace the cable. *(idea /3, why /2)*
3. A split pair can pass a basic connectivity check and light the link, but crosstalk causes errors at speed. *(idea /3, why /2)*
4. (1) link light / seated? (2) re-seat both ends (3) swap a known-good cable. *(idea /3, order /2)*

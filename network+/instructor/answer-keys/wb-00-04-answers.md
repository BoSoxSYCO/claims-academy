## Quiz answers (`quiz-00-04-knowledge-check.md`)

1. **A — the laptop.** It's your main computer and, later, your test tool.

2. **A — connects wired devices on the local network.** That is a switch's job. *(Not coffee, storage, or charging.)*

3. **A — connects your network to the internet.** That is the router's job.

4. **A — log into and configure.** A managed switch can be logged into — great for learning.

5. **A — RJ45.** The clip connector on an Ethernet cable. *(USB and HDMI are different connectors.)*

6. **False.** You don't need all the gear first — you can follow along on paper and add pieces slowly.

7. **A — the very first login/management.** The console port is management-only.

8. **Matching:** Router → C (door to the internet); Switch → A (connects wired devices); Ethernet cable → B (the wire between ports).

9. **link.** A link light shows a live connection on a port.

10. **Scenario —** It's a switch — it connects your wired devices so they can talk on the same local network; this one is a managed switch, so you can log in and set it up. *(Accept any answer that says a switch connects wired devices.)*

### Short answer (`wb-00-04-short-answer.md`) — model answers

2. The switch connects wired devices on the local network; a managed switch can be logged into and configured, which is ideal for learning.
3. It connects your local network to the internet and hands out addresses.

7. That the port has a live connection (a cable is connected and working).

### Vocabulary review (`wb-00-04-vocabulary-review.md`)
- **Switch** — a box that connects wired devices on the same local network.
- **Managed switch** — a switch you can log into and configure.
- **Router** — the device that connects your network to the internet.
- **Ethernet** — the family of rules for wired local networks.
- **RJ45** — the clip connector on the end of an Ethernet cable.
- **Console port** — a management-only port for the switch's first login.

**Part 2 (four pieces):** laptop · switch · router · Ethernet cables.
**Part 3:** 1. port · 2. link · 3. PowerConnect.

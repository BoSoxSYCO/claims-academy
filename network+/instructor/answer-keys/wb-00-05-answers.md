## Quiz answers (`quiz-00-05-knowledge-check.md`)

1. **A — near power, with room and tidy cables.** A calm, safe space prevents mistakes. *(Cramped, outlet-less, or drink-covered spaces cause problems.)*

2. **B — stop; it clicks in gently; check it's lined up.** Forcing bends the RJ45 pins.

3. **B — change only what you were told to.** A shared switch can lock others out if changed wrongly. *(Erasing/resetting/unplugging are all unsafe.)*

4. **A — the date, what you did, and what you saw.** That's the core of a lab-notebook entry.

5. **A — documentation.** A lab notebook is a simple form of documentation.

6. **False.** Keep drinks away — a spill can damage the gear and end your session.

7. **A — run a quick pre-lab checklist.** Confirm outlet, space, notebook, and which lab.

8. **Matching:** Label cables → C (know which is which); Tidy the space → B (cables don't get pulled); Keep a notebook → A (repeat or fix your setup).

9. **notebook.** A tidy workspace and a lab notebook make every lab smoother.

10. **Scenario —** Any two checks (outlet reachable, space clear, cables tidy, notebook open, which lab) plus one notebook line (the date, or "what I did / what I saw"). *(Accept any reasonable safe answer.)*

### Short answer (`wb-00-05-short-answer.md`) — model answers
1. A spot near power, with room to spread out, good light, and tidy cables.
2. Forcing bends the RJ45 pins and breaks the connection; it clicks in gently when lined up.
3. Change only what you were told to — a wrong change can lock other people out.
4. The date, what you did (the steps), and what you saw (link lights, results, anything odd).
5. It records what worked and what broke, so you can repeat or fix your setup and save hours.
6. So you know which cable goes where and avoid confusion as cables multiply.
7. That your outlet and space are ready, your notebook is open with the date, and you know which lab you're doing.

### Vocabulary review (`wb-00-05-vocabulary-review.md`)
- **Documentation** — the written record of what a network is and what you did to it.
- **Link light** — the LED on a port that glows when a connection is live.
- **RJ45** — the clip connector on the end of an Ethernet cable.
- **Console port** — a management-only port on the switch, for the first login.

**Part 2 (three columns):** date · what you did · what you saw.
**Part 3:** False · True · True.

### Hands-on observation (`wb-00-05-hands-on-observation.md`)
Learner's own setup. Check they placed the gear safely near power, tidied and labeled cables, and started a notebook with the date and three columns. Any switch-specific rule is `[TO VERIFY ON HARDWARE]`.

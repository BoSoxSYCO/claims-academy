## Quiz answers (`quiz-00-01-knowledge-check.md`)

1. **B — two or more devices connected to share and talk.** That is the definition of a network. *(A lone computer, a cable, or a phone brand are not networks.)*

2. **B — beginners.** "Entry-level" means it's designed for people starting out.

3. **B — work with any brand of equipment.** Vendor-neutral means not tied to one company's products.

4. **A — a phone streaming a video over the internet.** That's a network in daily use. *(A rock, notebook, or coffee are not networks.)*

5. **B — you have a skill, by passing an exam.** A certification is proof of a skill. *(It doesn't prove ownership or age.)*

6. **False.** No experience is needed — this course starts from zero and teaches every term.

7. **A — build a real home lab.** One of the three promises (teach concepts, build a home lab, get exam-ready). *(Cooking and phone sales are not promises; the course includes practice, not skips it.)*

8. **Matching:** Network → C (devices connected to share and talk); Certification → A (proves a skill by passing an exam); Vendor-neutral → B (works with any brand).

9. **router** (accept "modem" or "gateway"). The device that shares one internet connection.

10. **Scenario —** Encourage them: everyone starts as a beginner, no experience is needed, this course teaches every word slowly, you build a real lab with your hands, and you go at your own pace. *(Accept any warm, accurate answer that reflects "starts from zero, no experience needed.")*

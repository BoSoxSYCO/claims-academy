## How to study with them

1. Read the **Q** side and answer from memory (**active recall**) — don't peek.
2. Uncover the **A** side and check.
3. Sort each card into "got it" or "review again."
4. Re-drill the "review again" pile after a day, then a few days later (**spaced repetition**).
5. Focus on your weakest domains first (use your practice-exam results — see Lessons 15-04/15-05/15-06).

> These decks reinforce recall; they don't replace the lessons. Learn the concept first, then drill the facts.

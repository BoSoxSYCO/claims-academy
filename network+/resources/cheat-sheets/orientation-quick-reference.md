# Cheat Sheet — Orientation (Getting Started)

> A printable quick reference for **Module 00**. Grows as the module's lessons are published.

---

## Welcome — the big picture (Lesson 00-01)

- A **network** = two or more devices connected to share and talk.
- Your home already has one: the internet comes in, a **router** shares it, and your phone, laptop, and TV connect.
- A **certification** = proof of a skill, earned by passing an exam.
- **CompTIA Network+** = an entry-level, vendor-neutral networking certification (current exam **N10-009**).
  - *Entry-level* = made for beginners. *Vendor-neutral* = works with any brand.

### What this course promises

1. **Teach the concepts** — every term, slowly, with pictures.
2. **Build a real home lab** — connect real gear with your own hands.
3. **Get you exam-ready** — practice, quizzes, and a study plan.

### The mindset

- No experience needed. The course starts from zero.
- Go at your own pace — there is no clock.
- Everyone starts as a beginner. So do you, and that's exactly right.

> **Remember:** a network is just devices talking; Network+ proves you understand them; this course takes you there step by step.

---

## How the course works (Lesson 00-02)

- **Structure:** units → **modules** (00–15) → **lessons**.
- **Every lesson has the same 7 parts:** why it matters → key words → the idea (with a diagram) → see it for real → step by step → check yourself → remember this.
- **Each lesson comes with:** a workbook (practice), a quiz (answers in a separate key), and a cheat sheet.
- **Shared across the course:** one alphabetical glossary · two full practice exams · a flashcard deck · labs for hands-on work.

### The one-lesson routine

**Read** → **look** at the diagram → **do** the workbook → **take** the quiz (then check the key) → **keep** the cheat sheet handy.

- Do lessons **in order** — each builds on the last.
- Look up any fuzzy word in the **glossary** right away.
- It's fine to re-read. Go at your own pace.

> **Remember:** learn the pattern once — it never changes, so you can focus on learning networks.

# Workbook Templates

Reusable, printable worksheet templates for the NetworkAcademy+ lab manual.
The design standard they follow is [docs/WORKBOOK-SYSTEM.md](../../../docs/WORKBOOK-SYSTEM.md).

| Template | Type | Points |
|----------|------|:------:|
| `worksheet-fill-in-the-blank.md` | Fill-in-the-blank | 20 |
| `worksheet-matching.md` | Matching | 20 |
| `worksheet-label-the-diagram.md` | Label the diagram | 20 |
| `worksheet-draw-your-diagram.md` | Draw your own diagram | 25 |
| `worksheet-short-answer.md` | Short answer | 20 |
| `worksheet-hands-on-observation.md` | Hands-on observation | 25 |
| `worksheet-reflection.md` | Reflection (completion) | 10 |
| `worksheet-lab-documentation.md` | Formal lab report | 40 |
| `worksheet-vocabulary-review.md` | Vocabulary review | 20 |
| `worksheet-end-of-unit-review.md` | End-of-unit review | 100 |
| `tracker-progress.md` | Course progress tracker | — |
| `tracker-certification-readiness.md` | Certification readiness tracker | — |

**To build a worksheet:** copy the template, fill the front-matter, replace every
_italic placeholder_, keep the header/footer frame, and add a matching answer-key
entry in `instructor/answer-keys/`. Print-test in black & white before Publishing.
